IPTV should work on what you have at home. An app from the device's own app store is enough, and we tell you which one works best on your model.

Samsung and LG Smart TV

Most models from roughly 2018 and later run it with an app. Which apps exist depends on the model and year, so send us the model number.

Fire TV Stick and Android TV

The widest choice of apps and often the steadiest picture. A Fire TV Stick makes an older TV smart for a modest price.

Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

An IPTV player from the App Store, with the same app on all your Apple devices. Apple explains how to install apps on the Apple TV support pages.

MAG box and computer

A MAG box is linked to your account with the box's MAC address, and we do that setting for you. On a computer both VLC and IPTV apps work.

IPTV Norway, IPTV Nordic and IPTV Norge

IPTV Norway, IPTV Nordic and IPTV Norge: what is the difference?

The words are used interchangeably, but they mean slightly different things. This is how we usually explain it.

  • IPTV Norway and Norwegian IPTV: Norwegian channels with a Norwegian TV guide, for people who mainly want to watch Norwegian TV.
  • IPTV Nordic and Nordic IPTV: Norwegian channels plus channels from Sweden, Denmark and Finland. We cover it on the IPTV Nordic page.
  • IPTV Norge: the same thing in Norwegian. Our Norwegian site is written for people who search that way.

With IPTV Norway One both are in the same plan, so you do not have to choose between Norwegian and Nordic IPTV.

Questions and answers

Common questions about IPTV Norway

What is IPTV Norway?

IPTV Norway means television delivered over your internet connection instead of a dish, cable or aerial. With IPTV Norway One you watch Norwegian and Nordic channels in an app on your TV, box, phone or computer.

What is the difference between IPTV Norway and IPTV Nordic?

IPTV Norway is about Norwegian channels and a Norwegian TV guide. IPTV Nordic means that channels from Sweden, Denmark and Finland are included as well. With IPTV Norway One you get both in the same plan.

What internet speed do I need?

Plan for at least 10 Mbit/s per screen for HD and 25 Mbit/s for 4K. Measure on the TV or box itself, not on your phone. A network cable is almost always steadier than wifi.

Do I need to buy a box?

Usually not. Samsung and LG TVs from recent years run IPTV with an app. On an older TV, a Fire TV Stick or an Android TV box is the cheapest way to get started.

Does the subscription renew automatically?

No. We take no automatic payments and store no card details. When the period is ending you extend it yourself, if you want to.

How do I pay?

You order on WhatsApp and get the payment details there. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, and from other EU countries also SEPA, iDEAL, Bancontact and giropay.

Can I try it before buying a longer plan?

Yes. Start with one month for €15.99. It does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel. If it works well, you can take a longer plan when the month is over.

Is IPTV legal in Norway?

IPTV is just a technology, and it is legal. What matters is whether the channels you watch are licensed for distribution. We cannot give legal advice, so ask us what a plan includes before you order and read the information from Medietilsynet. You are responsible for how you use the service.
