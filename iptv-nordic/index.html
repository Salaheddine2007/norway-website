IPTV Nordic: four countries of TV in one app

If you follow television from more than one Nordic country, Nordic IPTV puts it in one place. Here is what it is, what you need and how it relates to IPTV Norway.

Short answer

IPTV Nordic means Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and Finnish channels in one IPTV app. With IPTV Norway One the Nordic channels are included in the same plan as the Norwegian ones, from €5.83 a month.

IPTV Nordic is a plain idea: one subscription and one app with channels from Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland. Nordic IPTV suits families with roots in more than one country, people who work across the border and anyone who likes to watch the neighbours' news, sport and series.

What IPTV Nordic includes

With IPTV Norway One the Nordic channels sit in the same plan as the Norwegian ones. That means Norwegian channels first, then channels from Sweden, Denmark and Finland, with a TV guide in the app. The line-up can change over time, so ask us about a specific channel before you order.

Nordic IPTV compared with IPTV Norway

  • IPTV Norway: Norwegian channels and a Norwegian guide. Read the IPTV Norway overview.
  • Nordic IPTV: the Norwegian channels plus Sweden, Denmark and Finland.
  • Norwegian version: the same information in Norwegian is on IPTV Nordic på norsk.

What you need to watch Nordic IPTV

You need an internet connection of at least 10 Mbit/s per screen for HD, and 25 Mbit/s for 4K, and a device with an app. Samsung and LG TVs, Android TV, Fire TV Stick, Apple TV, iPhone, Android and computers all work. The steps are in the guide to installing IPTV.

Who Nordic IPTV is for

Nordic IPTV is for households that follow television from more than one country. That can be a family with relatives in Sweden or Denmark, a couple where one grew up in Finland, or someone who commutes across the border and wants the local news in both places. It also suits people who simply enjoy comparing how the neighbouring countries make their news, sport and series.

How the TV guide works across countries

The TV guide (EPG) in the app lists what is on now and later for each channel, grouped by country. You move between countries in the channel list the same way you move between channel groups on a normal TV. If a programme time looks wrong, tell us the channel and we check the guide data with you.

What to expect from the channel line-up

The Nordic countries share a lot of culture, but each one has its own broadcasters and its own rights. That is why we never promise a list we cannot prove. Ask about the channels you care about before you order, and remember that the line-up can change over time. For background on how the Nordic countries cooperate, see the Nordic cooperation website.

What Nordic IPTV costs

The plans start at €15.99 for one month, and twelve months on one screen is €69.99, which is €5.83 a month. All prices for 1, 2 and 3 screens are on the IPTV subscription Norway page.

Questions about IPTV Nordic

What is IPTV Nordic?

Also called Nordic IPTV, it means TV over the internet with channels from all the Nordic countries in one app: Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland.

Is IPTV Nordic the same as IPTV Norway?

Not exactly. IPTV Norway focuses on Norwegian channels. Nordic IPTV adds the Swedish, Danish and Finnish ones. With IPTV Norway One both are in the same plan.

Can I ask about a specific channel?

Yes. Write to us on WhatsApp before you order and we tell you straight whether the channel is in the plan. The line-up can change over time.
