What 4K actually means for your picture

4K — also called Ultra HD or UHD — refers to a resolution of 3840 × 2160 pixels. That is four times as many pixels as a Full HD (1080p) picture. On a television larger than 50 inches, and especially when you sit within two or three metres of the screen, the difference is easy to see: edges are sharper, fine details stay clear, and text on screen is easier to read.

Pixel count is only part of the story. Modern 4K broadcasts also carry HDR (High Dynamic Range) information, which extends the range from the darkest shadow to the brightest highlight. HDR10 and Dolby Vision are the two most common HDR standards. When a 4K channel delivers HDR and your television supports it, colours look closer to what a camera actually recorded — more vivid in the highlights, with better depth in darker scenes.

For 4K IPTV to show its full quality, your screen must support 4K resolution and ideally one of those HDR standards. Most televisions sold in Norway since 2018 meet both requirements, but it is worth checking your model's specifications if you are not sure.

Compared with satellite or cable, 4K IPTV delivers the same resolution over a standard broadband connection. There is no need for a dish, a set-top box from a network provider, or a long-term service contract. The same subscription works on Smart TVs, streaming sticks, phones and computers without any extra hardware beyond what most households already have.

What your setup needs before you start

Three things need to work together smoothly for a good 4K IPTV experience: a fast and stable internet connection, a display that can show the full resolution, and an app or device that can decode the video stream efficiently.

Internet speed

A 4K stream typically uses between 15 and 25 Mbps, compared with roughly 4–8 Mbps for a standard HD channel. We recommend at least 25 Mbit/s per screen for 4K and at least 10 Mbit/s per screen for HD. If your household watches on two or three screens at the same time, make sure your total connection speed is enough for all of them together. A wired Ethernet connection is the most stable option for high-bitrate streams, but a Wi-Fi 5 or Wi-Fi 6 router in the same room usually works well too. Where possible, avoid running a 4K stream over a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi band shared with many other devices. Norwegian fibre and cable subscriptions typically offer 100 Mbit/s or more, which is well above what 4K IPTV on a single screen requires.

A compatible display

Check your television's specifications if you are unsure whether it supports 4K. The spec sheet or the sticker on the box will say "4K UHD" or "Ultra HD" if it does. An IPTV app on a Full HD (1080p) television will still receive a 4K stream, but the picture is downscaled to the screen's native resolution, so you will not see the extra detail. If you are planning to buy a new television, any mainstream model available in Norway today will be 4K.

An app that handles the right codec

4K channels from most IPTV services are encoded in H.265 (also called HEVC), which is roughly twice as efficient as the older H.264 codec. Your IPTV player must support H.265 decoding — preferably in hardware — to play these streams without buffering or frame drops. When you set up your subscription, the support team on WhatsApp can recommend the right app for your device and walk you through the initial configuration.

Smart TVs: Samsung, LG and others

Samsung and LG both offer IPTV-compatible apps in their app stores that support H.265 and handle 4K IPTV reliably on models from 2019 onwards. Samsung's Tizen platform and LG's webOS are both well-supported by the most popular IPTV players. You enter your subscription credentials — which you receive on WhatsApp after payment — and the full channel list with the built-in EPG appears immediately.

On older Samsung or LG models (before 2018), the built-in processor can sometimes struggle with H.265 decoding. In that case, connecting an external streaming stick to an HDMI port usually solves the problem. Samsung publishes setup guidance for its Smart TV range on the Samsung Norway support pages, which can be useful when you are installing a new app for the first time.

Once 4K IPTV is running on a Smart TV, the experience is close to a traditional broadcast service. The EPG shows the full schedule, you switch channels with the remote, and picture quality adjusts automatically to the highest the channel supports. You do not need to change any settings when moving from an HD channel to a 4K channel — the app handles that automatically.

Other Smart TV brands that run Android TV — including Sony and Philips — give you access to the Google Play Store, where several capable IPTV players with full 4K and HDR support are available for free. The process is the same: install the app, enter your login details, and you are set.

Streaming devices that work well with 4K IPTV

Dedicated streaming sticks and boxes often outperform a built-in Smart TV app for 4K IPTV, because they receive regular software updates and have faster processors. Here are the main options in Norway.

Fire TV Stick and Apple TV

The Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max is one of the most popular choices for IPTV in Norway. It decodes H.265 in hardware, handles 4K HDR streams without issue, and has a good selection of IPTV apps in the Amazon Appstore. The Apple TV 4K is another reliable option; it supports H.265 and Dolby Vision, and runs IPTV apps from the App Store. Apple publishes setup guidance at support.apple.com/apple-tv for adding and configuring apps.

Android TV and Google TV devices

The Chromecast with Google TV and similar Android TV devices have the widest selection of IPTV players. Google's support pages for Android TV at support.google.com/androidtv cover how to find and install apps from the Play Store. Most mid-range and higher Android TV boxes sold today handle 4K and H.265 without trouble. The MAG box is another common choice for IPTV and comes with a dedicated IPTV interface built in.

Computers, phones and tablets

Windows and Mac computers can run 4K IPTV through a desktop player such as VLC, provided the display and GPU support 4K output. iPhones, iPads and Android phones and tablets can also run IPTV apps and play 4K channels, though on a phone screen the extra resolution makes less visual difference. Casting or mirroring from a phone to a 4K television is a practical alternative if you do not want to install another app on the television itself.

Questions about 4K IPTV

Do I need a more expensive plan to get 4K IPTV?

No. Every IPTV Norway One plan includes 4K IPTV on the channels that broadcast in 4K. There is no premium tier or add-on required. The difference between plans is only the number of simultaneous screens and the subscription length.

Why is my 4K television not showing every channel in 4K?

Whether a channel appears in 4K depends on what that channel broadcasts, not on your subscription. Most channels transmit in HD; a smaller number transmit in 4K or Ultra HD. 4K IPTV means your subscription can deliver 4K where the channel supports it. The TV guide in the app shows what is currently available, and HD channels on a 4K TV still look very good.

How much internet speed do I need for 4K IPTV?

We recommend at least 25 Mbit/s per screen for 4K and at least 10 Mbit/s per screen for HD. If your home has two or three screens running at the same time, add up the requirements for each. Most Norwegian fibre and cable internet packages comfortably exceed these figures.

Can two screens watch 4K at the same time?

Yes, as long as you have a 2-screen or 3-screen plan and your connection is fast enough for both streams. Each screen runs its own app instance and can be on any channel independently — including channels that stream in 4K. The plans page has a full breakdown of the multi-screen options.

What if my device cannot play 4K streams properly?

The most common cause is that the app does not support H.265 (HEVC) hardware decoding. Switching to a different IPTV player — which the WhatsApp support team can recommend for your specific device — usually solves the problem. If the device itself is too old, connecting a modern streaming stick such as a Fire TV Stick 4K Max to an HDMI port on your television is a low-cost solution that works well.
