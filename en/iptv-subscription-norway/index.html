What an IPTV subscription Norway plan includes

Every IPTV subscription Norway plan has the same content: Norwegian and Nordic channels in HD, 4K where the channel broadcasts it, a TV guide and help with the installation on WhatsApp. The only things that change between plans are the length and the number of screens.

How to pay for your IPTV subscription

You order on WhatsApp and get the payment details there. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, and from other EU countries also SEPA, iDEAL, Bancontact and giropay. We do not store card details and nothing renews on its own.

Choosing an IPTV subscription Norway households can rely on

When you compare an IPTV subscription Norway sellers offer, look at the contract, the renewal and the support, not just the channel count. Our IPTV subscription Norway plans have no contract and no automatic renewal. Questions about plans

Can I change the number of screens later?

Yes. Write to us on WhatsApp before your period ends and say how many screens you want next time. The price follows the screen tier you choose.

What happens when the period is over?

The service stops. Nothing renews automatically, and we do not store your card details. If you want to continue, you order again.

Are prices shown with VAT?

The prices on this page are the prices you pay. We show euro with the Norwegian krone amount beside it. The krone amount is guidance based on a rate of about 11.7 NOK per euro.
